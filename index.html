Historical professional network · 2011–2020

Scottish Delirium Association

The Scottish Delirium Association was active from 2011 to 2020. It supported initiatives across Scotland, including delirium teaching and the development of clinical pathways. Many members contributed to SIGN 157, the national delirium guideline published in 2019. This website now provides key links and an archive of selected older materials.

Featured guideline

SIGN 157: Risk reduction and management of delirium

Scotland's national clinical guideline on delirium. Many Scottish Delirium Association members contributed to its development.
